# Milk Type metric artwork

These three transparent foreground PNGs are local to Milk Type analytics. They
do not replace the global Milk record icon or any other record-category asset.

Source: the user's approved `Milk Feedings Dashboard.png` reference supplied on
24 October 2026. The existing `assets/navbar/calendar-blue.png` is reused for
the date-filter trigger.

The built-in image editing tool isolated each reference icon separately:

- `milk-breastmilk.png`: glossy azure-blue milk droplet, bottom-left metric.
- `milk-formula.png`: glossy pink baby bottle, bottom-center metric.
- `milk-mixed.png`: glossy purple bottle with a droplet overlapping its left/front,
  bottom-right metric.

Prompt shared by all three edits: extract only the specified foreground object
from the approved screenshot; preserve its silhouette, proportions, colors,
smooth glossy 3D material, upper-left highlights and soft shading. Remove the
badge, card, text, numbers and all other objects. Center the isolated artwork
on a square genuinely transparent canvas with padding and clean antialiased
edges. Add no decoration or features; preserve the bottle teat and measurement
marks. The themed circular pastel badges are rendered by the component.

# Solid Foods metric artwork

Six transparent foreground PNGs (`food-fruits.png`, `food-vegetables.png`,
`food-grains.png`, `food-proteins.png`, `food-dairy.png`, `food-other.png`)
are local to `FoodCategoryIcon` / Solid Foods analytics. They do not change
the global record-icon registry.

Source: the user's approved `Pastel Solid Foods Dashboard.png` supplied on
24 October 2026. The built-in image editor isolated the green apple, orange
carrot, golden wheat, pink meat, blue-white milk carton and gray three dots.

Shared edit prompt: isolate only the specified foreground subject from the
approved screenshot; preserve silhouette, proportions, color, glossy rounded
3D material, upper-left reflection and gentle shading. Remove the circle badge,
card, labels, numbers and all other objects. Center on a square transparent
canvas with modest margin (artwork about 75% of the canvas), crisp antialiased
edges and no new features, glow, text or background. The application renders
the themed pastel circular surfaces separately, including blue for Dairy
and pink for Proteins.
